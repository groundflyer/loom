enum loom::vm::simd::ReconvMethod

Determines how compact_compiler executes threads.

Enumerator
WARP_FAIR 

Every PC in gang gets gets its time

HYBRID_MAJOR 

Only PCs having the majority are executed selecting between successors and warp search when utilization hits threshold

WARP_MAJOR 

Only PCs having the majority are executed selecting from warp

HYBRID_FAIR 

Prefers warp-major grouping, when utilization hits threshold, switch to fair

SUCCESSOR_FIRST 

Select first PC from successors falling back to pending set

SUCCESSOR_MAJOR 

Select PC based on the majority from successors falling back to pending set
